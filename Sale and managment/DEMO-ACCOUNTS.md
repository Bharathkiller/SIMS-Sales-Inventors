# Demo accounts

Use the sign-in page to select either workspace. The selected role fills its credentials automatically.

| Role | Email | Password | Access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Administrator | `admin@sims.com` | `admin123` | Dashboard, products, stock, sales, user management, and reports |
| Customer | `customer@sims.com` | `customer123` | Customer portal, product browsing, and order placement |

> This is a front-end demonstration. Credentials and role protection run in the browser and are not suitable for production use. A secure production deployment needs a backend authentication service and database.
